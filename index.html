Every brand deal, what it paid, what it moved.

Ink & Tally is an iPhone app for creators who do brand deals. Deliverables and deadlines, the fee and the commission each video actually earned, invoice chasing, a one-page media kit, and a coach that reads the contract and never names your price.

Get on the listSee how it works

A deal is a P&L line

Fee, commission per video, what's still owed. Import your TikTok Shop affiliate export and the money joins itself to the right deal.

Invoices that chase themselves

One status per deal: due, invoiced, paid. Reminders nudge you the day before a deadline and a week after an unpaid invoice.

Tracked deals pick up their own videos

Turn tracking on for a retainer and every new video that sells that brand's products attaches on import, marked auto-detected.

Contract review in plain English

Paste the agreement or import the PDF. You get a two-line verdict, the real problems in order of cost, and the sentence to send back for each.

Not in the App Store yet. Being built in the open by one creator. Your deals stay on your phone and in your own iCloud; the only thing that touches a server is a Coach request. Read the privacy policy.
